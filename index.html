Settings & controls
Left side of the screen drag to move: a little is a slow steady walk, most of the way a walk, all the way forward the low-ready run · right side drag to look (a finger on Fire, Aim or Ping turns the view too) · Fire on either side · Aim tap to stay up, hold for as long as you hold · Use hold to interact (lit when there's something to use) · Crouch tap, hold for prone · Reload tap, tap twice for a dump reload, hold to check the magazine · Nade the next grenade, then back to the gun · More ping (hold: comms wheel), shout, steady, lowered weapon, fire mode, inspect, backpack swap, night vision, mute, vote
Menus: long-press for what right-click and hover show · double-tap opens a pouch · drag to carry, a second finger turns what you carry · pinch to zoom the gunsmith and the sidearm web · on the route map, tap a node for its card and again to go. Sideways is the better way to play on a phone. A level goes fullscreen at the first tap; on an iPhone, Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open it from there.
WASD move · Space toggle lowered weapon · Shift slow & steady · C crouch · Q / E lean
LMB fire · RMB aim · MMB ping (hold MMB the comms wheel: light, laser, magnifier, calls to the squad) · R reload (R R dump reload, hold R check the magazine, or the chamber on a gun without one) · I inspect (hold I hold the chamber check open; the mouse turns what you hold) · 1 / 2 / wheel swap (wheel while aiming: magnification)
3 / 4 / 5 a grenade into your hand (frag and flashbang to start; drag one onto a key's box in the inventory to change it): hold LMB to pull the pin, release to throw, RMB underhand / weak / strong; the key again or 1 / 2 puts it away · H body & kit (as Tab) · hold B swap with the gun in your backpack · V shout compliance · X fire selector · N night vision down / up (on your helmet; also on the comms wheel), Shift+N its IR illuminator · T a thermal sight's palette
hold F doors (aim high: open, low: kick), bag evidence, arrest, restrain or revive
Y vote to leave · M mute · F8 echo paths · F9 hitboxes · Tab inventory, health & scoreboard · Enter chat · Esc menu

Controller (Xbox names): LS move, further is faster · RS look · L3 slow & steady on / off · R3 shout · RT fire · LT aim · hold A interact · B crouch (hold: prone) · X reload · Y swap (aiming: magnification) · LB ping (hold: comms wheel, RS picks) · tap RB next grenade · D-pad up night vision, down lowered weapon, left / right lean · View inventory · Menu pause
hold RB with: A vote to leave · B body & kit · X fire selector · Y backpack swap · Up IR illuminator · Down thermal palette · Left inspect · Right mute · View chat
menus: D-pad select · LS cursor · RS scroll · A confirm (hold and move: carry) · B back · X more (right-click) · Y turn what you carry · LB / RB tabs
