# Working Households - Apartment Personas

Three ordinary adult households. NEW marks anything beyond the existing furniture list or the wood/tile/carpet floor kinds.

## Priya Nair - night-shift nurse who lives by blackout curtains
- **Who:** 34, ICU nurse on 7pm-7am rotations, lives alone, four years in the flat. Sleeps in daytime and keeps a pillbox of vitamins and a thermos by the door like a ritual.
- **Home type:** Single-floor (upstairs). Hallway, living, kitchen, bedroom, bathroom (NEW).
- **Mood:** Quiet, tired, tidy
- **Palette:**
  - Living: wall #8fa391 (sage green), ceiling #d6d9cf (pale grey-green), trim #3f4f45 (pine)
  - Kitchen: wall #c9d3c4 (mint wash), ceiling #dfe2d8 (milk), trim #5d7a68 (fern)
  - Bedroom: wall #4d5a52 (dark moss, painted for daytime sleep), ceiling #8a9489 (dusk grey), trim #1f2622 (near black)
  - Bathroom (NEW): wall #b9cfc9 (clinic teal), ceiling #e4ece8 (white), trim #7d9c96 (steel teal)
- **Floors:** Living: carpet #6b6f66 (grey-green, flat weave). Kitchen: lino NEW #a8a592 (worn oatmeal). Bedroom: carpet #3d423e (charcoal). Bathroom: tile #cfd8d6 (pale grey).
- **Furniture:**
  - Living: one small two-seat sofa, dark teal #35564f, faded on the arm; tv small and low; one tall plant, healthy; lamp with warm shade.
  - Kitchen: counter clear apart from a kettle; fridge white and bare of magnets; dining table pushed against the wall as a one-person table.
  - Bedroom: bed with navy bedding, always made on the pillow side only; wardrobe closed, one scrubs sleeve poking out; sidetable with pill organiser and lamp.
  - Bathroom (NEW): bath/shower, sink, towel rail.
- **What they own:** Thick blackout curtain, charcoal #26292b, floor to ceiling, in the bedroom; scrubs on a hook behind the door, ceil blue #3b7fa6, one set; a lanyard and badge on the hallway hook, white card on a red cord; a stack of nursing textbooks, 0.5m tall, orange and white spines; an oversized insulated lunch bag, 40cm, navy; a tall thermos, 30cm, steel; a yoga mat rolled in the living corner, plum #6e4a6b.
- **Signs of life:** Coffee mug rings on the sidetable, a plate left in the sink, a laundry basket of scrubs, shoes lined by the door, one sock on the radiator. Dinner is cereal at 6pm.
- **In a raid:** Home but only just awake, lights off in the bedroom, kitchen light on, kettle warm, her phone alarm just went. Complies instantly with hands visible and narrates calmly, "I'm a nurse, I'm unarmed."

- **Mood:** Busy, warm, cluttered
- **Palette:**

  - Kitchen: wall #d8b45e (mustard), ceiling #eadfc2 (butter), trim #8a3d2a (brick)

- **In a raid:** Marcus asleep on the sofa in work trousers, Dani still at the mezzanine desk with a laptop open. He shouts, "wrong address, I deliver parcels", while she raises both hands and keeps calling the officers' attention to the stairs.

- **Palette:**

  - Kitchen: wall #c8c6b8 (dishwater beige), ceiling #dcdad0 (smoke), trim #8b8776 (stone)

- **Floors:** Living: laminate NEW #8c8579 (grey-oak). Kitchen: lino NEW #7e8a8e (chequered grey, peeling). Bedroom A: wood #6b5a45. Bedroom B: carpet #2b2838 (dark purple-grey). Bathroom: tile #b8c0bd.
- **Furniture:**
  - Living: sofa cheap grey #5c6168 with one saggy cushion; tv large (Elliot's); armchair brown vinyl #6e4b34 (Tomasz's, facing away); sidetable with remotes.
  - Kitchen: counter split by a masking-tape line; fridge two shelves labelled; bin overflowing.
