# Long-term older tenants

## Harold Pruitt - widower who keeps every room exactly as Margaret left it
- **Who:** 81, retired bus driver, lives alone, 46 years in this flat. Still sets two cups out every morning and only ever uses one.
- **Home type:** single-floor. Living, kitchen, bedroom, dining (used as a shrine to the past, never eaten at), bathroom (NEW).
- **Mood:** preserved, hushed, nicotine-yellow
- **Palette:**
  - Living: wall #d6c68c (faded mustard wallpaper), ceiling #d9d0b0 (smoke-stained cream), trim #7a4e2d (varnished brown)
  - Kitchen: wall #c9d3b0 (pale sage), ceiling #d9d0b0 (smoke-stained cream), trim #f0ead6 (yellowed white)
  - Dining: wall #b98a5e (tobacco tan), ceiling #d9d0b0 (smoke-stained cream), trim #5a3a22 (dark walnut)
  - Bedroom: wall #cdb3a6 (dusty peach), ceiling #d9d0b0 (smoke-stained cream), trim #8a5a5a (faded rose)
  - Bathroom (NEW): wall #9fc2b8 (seafoam tile), ceiling #e4dfd0 (old white), trim #3f6f66 (bottle green)
- **Floors:** living: carpet #6b4a3a (worn brown); kitchen: lino (NEW) #b0a070 (mustard check); dining: parquet (NEW) #8a6038 (honey herringbone); bedroom: carpet #8a6a6a (dusty mauve); bathroom: tile #7fa89d (seafoam).
- **Furniture:** Living: sofa in mustard-brown with a crocheted throw, one armchair worn bald (his), tv small and boxy on a sidetable, lamp with beige shade. Kitchen: fridge in almond, counter with canisters. Dining: dining table under a lace cloth, nothing used. Bedroom: bed made on one side only, wardrobe closed, dresser topped with photos. NEW: bedside radio, glass display cabinet.
- **What they own:** a glass-fronted china cabinet (1.2 x 1.8 m, walnut with white plates); Margaret's sewing machine on a treadle table (0.9 m, black and gold); a tall mantel clock (0.4 m, brown); an orange vinyl armchair, hers, untouched (0.8 m); a bus-company enamel sign (0.4 m, red and white); a rubber plant gone leggy (1.6 m, dark green); a stack of TV Guides (0.3 m, glossy yellow).
- **Signs of life:** cup rings on the sidetable, one set of slippers by the armchair, calendar stuck on October 2019, dust on every surface except his chair.
- **In a raid:** He is asleep in the bedroom on his side of the bed, hearing aids on the nightstand. He wakes confused, assumes burglars, and calls for Margaret before raising his hands.

## Stanislaw "Stan" Kowalczyk - retired machinist who turned the spare room into a shop
- **Who:** 74, retired toolmaker, lives with his cat-less quiet, 38 years here. Wipes his hands on a rag tucked in his belt even when sitting down to eat.
- **Home type:** loft (ground floor, open upper). Living (double height), kitchen, bedroom on the gallery, office as workshop.
- **Mood:** greasy, orderly, stubborn
- **Palette:**
  - Living: wall #7f9a85 (institutional green), ceiling #d2d0c0 (shop grey-white), trim #3a4a40 (dark conduit green)
  - Kitchen: wall #b5b8ae (putty grey), ceiling #d2d0c0 (shop grey-white), trim #c99a2e (machine-tool yellow)
  - Bedroom: wall #8f949c (steel grey), ceiling #d2d0c0 (shop grey-white), trim #5a5f66 (gunmetal)
  - Office/workshop: wall #6f7f78 (dull slate-green), ceiling #bfbdb0 (oil-dulled grey), trim #b8402a (toolbox red)
- **Floors:** living: wood #6a5844 (scuffed oak); kitchen: lino (NEW) #8a8a80 (grey speckle); bedroom: carpet #55606a (flat blue-grey); workshop: concrete (NEW) #7a7770 (oil-stained slab).
- **Furniture:** Living: one huge armchair in cracked brown leather, tv on a milk crate, bookshelf of manuals. Kitchen: counter with a kettle and a hot plate, fridge with magnets. Bedroom: narrow bed, military-neat, wardrobe. Workshop: desk replaced by a NEW lathe and NEW workbench, files cabinets stuffed with drawings.
- **What they own:** a bench lathe (1.2 m long, grey-green); a red rolling toolbox (0.8 m, red); pegboard of outlined tools (1.5 x 1 m, off-white); a bench vise (0.3 m, blue-grey); a coffee can of bolts (0.2 m, silver); a drill press (1.6 m, grey and yellow); a hanging work lamp on a spring arm (0.7 m, black).
- **Signs of life:** swarf on the floor, cardboard sheets under the lathe, half-finished brass part clamped mid-cut, a sandwich in wax paper on the bench.
- **In a raid:** Lights are on in the workshop, he is still up, and the radio is playing polka. He turns off the lathe first, then raises his hands, grumbling that nothing here is worth stealing.

  - Office: wall #3f5f7a (library blue), ceiling #cfc8ba (plain cream), trim #a8742a (brass)
- **Floors:** living: carpet #7a2f3a (Persian red, NEW rug pattern layered over wood #5a4632); dining: parquet (NEW) #7a5230 (dark herringbone); kitchen: tile #c8b890 (cream and brown check); bedroom: carpet #a89098 (faded rose); office: wood #4a3a2c (dark stain).
