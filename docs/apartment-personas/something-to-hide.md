# Apartment personas: the tenants with something to hide

## Dario Vance and Kell Adeyemi - a crew's stash flat, rented in a cousin's name and slept in by nobody who lives there
- **Who:** Dario 29, Kell 33. No front job. Two men on a rota, three weeks in a flat leased to a name neither answers to. Telling habit: they never sit with their back to the door and never turn the main light on, only a bare bulb on the cooker hood.
- **Home type:** Single-floor (upstairs). Living room, kitchen, one bedroom, bathroom (NEW).
- **Mood:** Hollow, jumpy, temporary
- **Palette:**
  - living: wall #8f9a88 (sick sage), ceiling #b9b9ab (nicotine grey), trim #5c5f55 (dirty olive)
  - kitchen: wall #a7a58c (stale khaki), ceiling #b9b9ab, trim #3f4038 (soot)
  - bedroom: wall #8f9a88, ceiling #b9b9ab, trim #5c5f55
  - bathroom (NEW): wall #9fb0a6 (chipped mint), ceiling #b9b9ab, trim #3f4038
- **Floors:** living: bare concrete (NEW) #7a7873 with a paint-spattered patch; kitchen: torn lino (NEW) #8c7a55 curling at edges; bedroom: bare concrete (NEW) #7a7873; bathroom: tile #b8c0b8, one square missing.
- **Furniture:** Living: one sofa, split and taped, #4b4a47; one armchair turned to face the door; tv on the floor, no stand, screen angled at the entry. No lamp, bare bulb. Kitchen: fridge #d8d4c8 with the door taped shut, counter bare, bin overflowing. Bedroom: no bed or dresser, just a mattress (NEW) 190x90 cm, #6a6f78 stained, on the floor, plus a second one propped on its side against the window (NEW). Wardrobe with doors removed, empty.
- **What they own:**
  - Sports bags (NEW), six, 70 cm long, black and red, stacked in the wardrobe.
  - Plastic storage tubs (NEW), eight, 60x40 cm, grey with white lids, towered in the kitchen.
  - Foil-covered window (NEW), full pane, silver.
  - Camping lantern (NEW), 25 cm, orange, on the floor.
  - Pizza boxes, about a dozen, flat white and red, in a stack 80 cm tall.
  - Folding table (NEW), 120 cm, beige plastic, with a scatter of small bags.
  - Padlocked toolbox (NEW), 50 cm, yellow.
- **Signs of life:** Takeaway cartons on every surface, cigarette ends in a mug, one sleeping bag still zipped around nobody, phone chargers snaking from one socket, a cheap phone box dropped open.
- **In a raid:** Kell is on the mattress by the window with the lantern off; Dario sits in the armchair facing the door with the sofa as cover. They freeze, then bolt for the hallway in different directions and fight only when cornered.

## Marguerite "Mags" Delacroix - a fence whose rooms are stacked with other people's televisions
- **Who:** 54. Runs a "second-hand and clearance" shop on paper. Lives alone, eleven years in the flat. Telling habit: she reads the sticker on the back of every new box before she says hello.
- **Home type:** Loft (ground floor, double height), open upper floor used as overflow. Living, kitchen, dining, bedroom, office (upper).
- **Mood:** Crammed, warm, watchful
- **Palette:**
  - living: wall #d9b25c (butter mustard), ceiling #e6dcc0 (cream), trim #7a3b2a (brick brown)
  - kitchen: wall #c9884a (burnt orange), ceiling #e6dcc0, trim #4a2f22 (dark walnut)
  - dining: wall #d9b25c, ceiling #e6dcc0, trim #7a3b2a
  - bedroom: wall #b46a5a (dusty terracotta), ceiling #e6dcc0, trim #4a2f22
  - office: wall #c9884a, ceiling #e6dcc0, trim #4a2f22
- **Floors:** living: carpet #7d3f35 oxblood, worn path to the door; kitchen: tile #c8b88e; dining: wood #6b4a30; bedroom: carpet #7d3f35; office: wood #6b4a30.
- **Furniture:** Living: three tvs (the existing tv, pushed against each other) plus a stack of boxed ones; sofa #8a3b2d pushed to the wall to free floor; two armchairs holding cartons. Dining: table #5e3e26 covered with a bedsheet and heaped goods, no chairs usable. Kitchen: normal, spotless, the only tidy room. Bedroom: bed #a35a50 with boxes underneath. Office (upper): desk and files, a shelving bookshelf of small boxed gadgets.
- **What they own:**
  - Flat-screen tvs (NEW), twelve, 100x60 cm, black, stacked on edge.
  - Laptop boxes (NEW), thirty, 40x30 cm, white and silver.
  - Bicycles (NEW), four, 170 cm, mixed colours, hung off the upper rail.
  - Speakers (NEW), tall pair, 120 cm, black with orange cones.
  - Gold-framed mirror (NEW), 150 cm, gold.
  - Stuffed holdall, 80 cm, tartan, always packed by the door.
  - Label gun on a nail, hand-sized, bright blue.
- **Signs of life:** Price tags on the cat, mismatched stickers on the wall, a kettle on constantly, a notebook of numbers under a mug, the smell of cardboard and tea.
- **In a raid:** Mags sits at the dining table in a dressing gown behind a wall of boxes, hands on the sheet. She talks loudly and politely, stalls, and will hurl a lamp or box to block the hallway before she complies.
